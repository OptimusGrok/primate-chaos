Room: PUBLIC · share the link to play together
How to play (Meta Quest): no joysticks. Put your hands on the ground and push off to move, like a gorilla. Swing your arms backwards to run, push down hard to jump.
Climb: push against trees, rocks and the lower rock wall, or hold grip to grab and pull yourself up. You can climb the lower 9 m of the mountain walls.
Vines: hold grip on a vine to swing. Let go to fly. The cave shaft vine and the cliff vine are grabbed with the triggers (either hand).
Test build: hold A to charge a jump (full at 2 s, hands on the ground), let go to leap — or it jumps by itself at 2 s. Find the tunnel at the back of the upper wall cave. A ceiling shaft in the wide cave climbs out to a mountain-top clearing. Walls 3 and 4 (south and west) are ocean cliffs. Press Y (left controller) for the menu: sound, debug, labels, restart, exit, resume.
Tag: lava monkeys are IT. Touch others with a hand to infect them. When everyone is lava, a new round starts.
Bananas: grab them for points. Canopy course: climb the tree with the sign to the start pad, then race to the finish.
Wrist: look at your left wrist for bananas and timer. Tap the red button with your right hand to mute your mic, or the DEBUG button (left of the panel) to show the debug overlay (also: ?debug=1).
Drag/click: look · W A S D: swing arms · Space: jump · hold E: climb/grab vine · hold F: charge jump · M: mute · `: debug · L: labels · Y: menu (1-6) · Esc: exit
