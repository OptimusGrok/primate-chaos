PRIMATE CHAOS

Swing, climb and tag your way around the jungle.


Room: PUBLIC · share the link to play together
How to play (Meta Quest): no joysticks. Put your hands on the ground and push off to move, like a gorilla. Swing your arms backwards to run, push down hard to jump.
Climb: push against trees, rocks and the lower rock wall, or hold grip to grab and pull yourself up. The upper wall is too smooth to climb.
Vines: hold grip on a vine to swing. Let go to fly.
Charged jump: hold A to charge a jump (full at 3 s, hands on the ground), let go to leap, or it jumps by itself at 3 s.
The cave: find the tunnel at the back of the upper wall cave. It opens onto a dark chasm with climbable pillars, then a side tunnel out onto the cliff. The cliff vine is grabbed with the triggers (either hand); a trigger closes your hand.
Menu: press Y (left controller) for sound, restart, exit or resume.
Tag: lava monkeys are IT. Touch others with a hand to infect them. When everyone is lava, a new round starts.
Bananas: grab them for points. Canopy course: climb the tree with the sign to the start pad, then race to the finish.
Wrist: look at your left wrist for bananas and timer. Tap the red button with your right hand to mute your mic.
Drag/click: look · W A S D: swing arms · Space: jump · hold E: climb/grab vine · hold F: charge jump · M: mute · Y: menu (1-4) · Esc: exit
